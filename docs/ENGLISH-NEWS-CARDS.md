# English game cards

Replaces the Our Things layout with equal landscape cards based on the parent task's direct inspection of the user's TSM news-section reference. Three columns on desktop, two on tablet, one on mobile; 18px gaps, small corner radii, image backgrounds, lower black gradients, and white left-aligned titles. Only the four verified games are shown. Category labels distinguish original games from the external game used in class.

The hub's navigation, purposes, categories, actions, and sorting are in English. Game internals and screenshots are unchanged. Results and rules links retain accurate action labels. The existing hero composition and artwork are preserved with a bottom fade into the black page background.

Chrome validation at 1280 × 1000 and 390 × 844: four cards, loaded images, three/one columns, no horizontal or text overflow. A–Z and Featured sorting work. Screenshots: `news-hero-desktop.jpg`, `news-cards-desktop.jpg`, `news-cards-mobile.jpg`.

No private services, backend data, credentials, or game runtimes were changed.
